### Proposed next slice shape: scoped governance deferral

This heading retains the original shape's navigation anchor. The outcome below
is now implemented at `8b13505`, with aggregate closeout and human recap complete;
it is no longer awaiting dispatch. Its completed slice owns the evidence/limits.

Name: **`scoped-governance-deferral`**. Outcome: for one newly registered local
project, record human-approved deliberate deferral of the existing Contracts
freshness-policy admission scope and resolve that exact current scope state through
a typed Rust SDK consumer after restart. No default configuration activates.
This is an observable governance-lifecycle outcome, not delivery of full independent
contract evaluation or a universal applicability decision for every project scope.

For the selected defer-first journey it supplies the positive current deferred
basis consumed by later first establishment on the same identity, which no current
setup path records. Positive applicability resolution is the general prerequisite
for dependent independent use: deferral, established non-applicability and satisfied
applicable qualification are alternative G4 bases. Absence of governance files
supplies none of them. Separate delivery of deferral and later establishment is a
planning choice, not an extra semantic requirement. Choosing full independent
evaluation now would combine unresolved conformance, permitted use, operating
authority and evaluation closure. The existing standalone credential-backed path
shows useful native mechanisms; it does not justify bypassing them.

**Concrete workflow:** the skill-guided agent inspects the repository and discusses
Charter/posture, governance and delegated local work with the human. After the human
approves the exact initial choices, the agent or another typed caller submits the
scoped deferral instruction. The native governance responsibility registers P and
commits its exact deferred basis; the SDK returns attributable scope-state truth.
Reopening resolves the same P/scope/basis. This increment records the scoped
initialization decision only: Charter/posture authoring and operating delegation
must use their actual supported owner paths and cannot be claimed complete from
this outcome. A subsequent contract consumer can use the basis only after closing
every governance scope and independent-use prerequisite relevant to its operation.

**Meaningful refusal:** a caller offers an ordinary initialized root with no
positive registration history, or deletes an established project's active record
and asks to defer. Neither becomes a fresh deferred project. Missing, corrupt,
substituted or uncertain required state refuses/retains uncertainty; inspection of
content does not imply executable permission.

The [completed slice](slices/scoped-governance-deferral/README.md) makes these
observable boundaries concrete within its exact committed scope:

1. Exact human-approved project/scope instruction and advisory provenance are
   retained with a committed positive deferral; no configuration, project
   qualification, contract permission or action authority is fabricated.
2. Native and typed SDK resolution/replay after restart preserve the same identity,
   scope and basis, distinguish current resolution from historical replay, and
   never infer deferral from absence. Established, deferred, refused/unavailable
   and uncertain outcomes remain distinguishable through explicit typed results;
   current scope state remains separate from request execution/delivery.
3. Competing initialization, root/identity substitution, conflicting identity reuse,
   lost replies and interrupted persistence cannot report a false committed deferral
   or permit ordinary retry of an uncertain effect. Refresh/reset preserves retained
   scope history. Existing established governance remains binding and recoverable.
4. The exact current deferral basis remains available for later first establishment
   on P. That later transition is not implemented here; stale, consumed or uncertain
   state cannot be asserted eligible. No old setup root is migrated or backfilled.
5. The selected scope is explicitly bounded. Its deferral cannot authorize another
   scope or direct checks requiring other unresolved qualification/authority facts.
   Current Contracts lifecycle, policy registration and evaluation outcomes are not
   relabeled as HR-007-compliant by this increment.

The existing Contracts project-admission-governance responsibility owns scope-state
validation, identity/history, current resolution and committed truth; Engine retains
repository identity/durability and operating authority. SDK composes and projects
owner outcomes. Preserve Contracts JSON and versioned identities; the
[format standard](structured-data-format-standard.md) still awaits human review
and supplies no migration approval. Retained owner history must survive state reset.
The exact rule evolution, records/concurrency/recovery mechanics, APIs and scope
representation are selected in the separate slice plan, not this program shape. No
new ordinary capability, CLI/Tauri parity or universal governance service is selected.
Later freshness configuration and evaluation age limits remain caller-supplied;
86,400 seconds is an example, with no implicit 24-hour or other policy default.

**Prerequisites:** HR-007 semantic approval and the inspected identity/durability,
fresh-project owner and typed SDK mechanisms are complete foundations. Positive
deferral registration/resolution and its narrow initialization coordination landed
inside this completed slice at `8b13505`. That completed authorization and closeout
do not authorize later establishment or independent-use work. Full independent checks
also need supported owner conformance/permitted use, every applicable qualification,
initial operating authority/delegation, complete direct requirements and evaluation
closure; those are incomplete and are not prerequisites for merely recording deferral.

**Review boundary:** return the first observable committed deferral/resolution/refusal
outcome to human review under the lifecycle. The selected Packet-1 boundary
coincided with slice completion; meta closeout and the human recap are complete.
The separately authorized later-establishment preparation returns to meta before
product implementation.

## How to read coverage

**Already covered** means the bounded behavior exists in inspected source and test
bodies. **Needs changing** means a current behavior cannot serve the approved role
unchanged. **Missing** means absent from the inspected plausible owners, execution
paths and public declarations. **Unverified** means the evidence does not establish
the claim. None is a fresh runtime certification. A whole area can contain all four.

The [research index](../../research/hcm-coverage-landing-2026-10-01/README.md)
provides the source hierarchy, complete subsection/scenario dispositions, stable
claim IDs, evidence paths and limitations. The five areas below are provisional
capability groupings. They are not five implementation packets.
