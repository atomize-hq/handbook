## Responsibilities retained

The following names identify semantic responsibilities, not selected physical
components. Co-location may be chosen later without merging their propositions.

| Responsibility | Truth it establishes |
| --- | --- |
| Authoritative work-state/structure owner | Item identity, exact composition bindings, state, relations and coherent finite cuts; authorized changes and committed successor state/receipts. |
| Relevant definition/admission owners | Supported composition and kind meaning, immutable versions, compatibility and applicable qualification; establishment does not assign governance. Subsequent bounded Engine composition/kind delivery is recorded in the [implemented baseline](../../implemented-baseline.md); it does not establish broader qualification or governing completion. |
| Work Progression | Independently resolved governing completion, exact target/relation and result accounting, local/parent policies, current qualification under G5, pure eligibility/disposition, refusal and replay. |
| Contracts responsibilities | Exact complete definitions and requirements, assessment occurrence, evidence/claim/metric/gate truth; assessment does not establish work state. |
| Engine responsibilities | Applicable posture and independent operating/evaluation authority, exact historical validity and declared current verification. |
| Action authority, executor, and state/effect owner | Separately attributable authorization, attempt/terminal, guarded effect and durable commit receipt. Eligibility grants none of these by implication. |

Work Specifications remain authored plans. Their acceptance prose, approval,
checkboxes, references or `completed` labels cannot establish operational truth.
A later revision may reference issued outcomes; a mutation cannot silently rewrite
the earlier input revision. See the [Work Specification boundary](../operational-reliance-and-actions.md#work-specification-relationship).

## Selected kinds and hierarchy

**Optional immutable versioned work kinds** identify reusable owner-supported
semantic compositions and their compatibility contracts. Explicit per-item
composition remains valid. A kind adds no primitive behavior or authority and
does not require a separate registry if an equivalent whole-composition mechanism
is later selected. It belongs to the semantic-definition category, not a seventh
authored artifact family, generic Artifact Kind, or Action Kind.

Per-item variation is limited to declared supported bounds. A different
composition requires a supported variant or explicit composition; it cannot
silently override the meaning of an unchanged kind binding. Role composition,
lifecycle selection, relation participation and exact policy references are
limited to the supported semantic menu. Kinds do not select actual members,
assessment targets or governing assignments, provide arbitrary hooks/rules,
or introduce programmable transitions or a universal workflow engine.

Vocabulary and supported levels are configurable, with no universal fixed
taxonomy or depth. A Feature may contain many Phases, each many Slices, each many
Tasks/Packets. Supported compositions may omit levels or combine roles. Names
alone do not determine completion meaning, authority, or the repository's
engineering slice/packet lifecycle.

The **initial native containment boundary** is same-project, with at most one
containment parent per item and finite declared cuts. Breadth and nesting do not
require several parents. Multiple direct same-project prerequisites may cross
branches and Features. Containment, prerequisites, grouping, informational
references, and ordering retain separate meanings; membership or display order
does not automatically create a gate, authorization, or scheduler.

Genuine multi-parent containment and cross-project/external prerequisite/effect
capabilities remain later, expressly versioned choices. A shared prerequisite is
not joint constituent responsibility. Preserve one exact shared identity rather
than fabricating duplicates to fit containment. Initial support promises neither
arbitrary graphs nor a configurable-many-parents switch; unsupported relations
and reach must be disclosed and the affected operation refused.

## Selected contract roles and completion distinctions

Three roles remain distinct:

| Role | Meaning and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Required contract output | When declared for planning work, this role requires an exact owner-established complete contract and compatible definition closure. Before issuance, record the output question, not a guessed future exact reference. |
| Exact requirements input | Implementation consumes a particular contract revision through a supported input/coverage role. A link alone establishes neither qualification nor governing assignment. |
| Assessment-result input | A consuming policy accepts exact owner-produced assessment truth for a declared target, proposition, coverage and currentness. A contract is not its result. |

Planning may produce C@1 and implementation consume it. Intrinsic completeness,
project qualification, governing assignment, actual required review, assessment,
eligibility and committed closure are separate propositions. Producing or
validating C@1 does not close the authoring work; that work retains its own
applicable completion requirements. Evaluating C@1 against implementation differs
from assessing C@1 as subject data. Neither entails a mandatory recursive chain
of validation contracts.

Every evaluation preserves the complete contract-rooted expected-claim inventory
and declared applicability/accounting. Exact supported per-target coverage relates
bounded work questions to assessment roles; it cannot select favorable claims or
silently remove expected claims. Unsupported coverage refuses. Child coverage
does not establish a parent's integrated outcome.

Independent authoring, intrinsic completeness validation and supported direct
evaluation require no work item, kind registration or skill pack. Direct evaluation
still needs positive governance-applicability resolution, any applicable project
qualification, complete compatible definitions/intent, independent Engine
authority, capability/readiness and evidence/truth closure under G4/G5. Deliberate
deferral is positive owner truth, not an absent file or damaged governance.
An independent result answers its exact question without claiming governed
delivery was assessed. No universal human approval per contract is introduced.

Governed completion retains the [independently established relationship](../operational-reliance-and-actions.md#independently-governed-completion):
actual project/effect/target/scope, governing assignment, complete requirements,
adopted supported protocol, exact assessment roles/coverage and present reliance.
The mandatory positive role needs a Decision-seeking eligible contract and
original `decision_seeking` intent. Diagnostic findings may serve permitted
supplemental/remediation roles; they cannot substitute for that role.

## Selected applicability, authority, and exception rules

Applicability follows **explicitly authorized actual project, effect, target and
scope rules**, including declared finite descendant reach. Containment and labels
do not implicitly inherit obligations. A parent contributes descendant obligations
only through its authorized scope rule. Parent-only integration requirements stay
parent-only, while separately assigned descendant obligations apply where their
actual predicates match. Moving or renaming work cannot silently erase a
still-applicable obligation.

Compatible obligations apply cumulatively through supported composition. Retain
each exact source, role, target/scope, population, process condition and currentness
truth. Kind-associated references and item selections must conform to this
independently resolved governance; they cannot waive it. Complete accounting does
not mean blending gates, collapsing distinct roles, or inventing a merged policy.
Repeated display names do not establish duplicate semantic obligations.

There is no universal strictest-policy order, nearest-parent winner or arbitrary
policy algebra. Unresolved applicability, incompatible/unsupported composition,
missing required formation facts or incomplete boundaries refuse the dependent
formation. A valid adverse assessment follows its named outcome policy rather
than being disguised as malformed formation or silently overridden. Exact policy
contents and outcome mappings remain later choices.

Initial project/governance decisions and delegation follow G2. Supported routine
operations may proceed under that prior authority without another human prompt
solely because a different owner performs a check. Reserved decisions, unresolved
material ambiguity and missing authority without an authorized resolution still
require escalation. Required review must actually occur, under its exact decision
authority, independence/composition and assurance rules; a configured agent role
or conversational assertion supplies none of those facts by itself.

Changes and exceptions use prior authorized bounded mechanisms. New terms cannot
authorize their own activation. A typed exception needs its named/versioned
policy, separately authorized/admitted exact record, scope, effect and currentness;
only that bounded effect may apply. It cannot fabricate past review or authority,
rewrite an assessment, widen the same contract's permitted use or remove the
mandatory governed-completion floor. Failure supplies no automatic waiver.
Operating authority, Work Progression decisions, action authorization and actual
state-owner effects remain independently attributable.

## Selected relation timing, accounting, and closure

A prerequisite names the **guarded operation and required proposition**: start,
close, or both, as supported. Direction, exact target/state/result role, scope,
cut and currentness must be declared. Candidate existence is not completed work;
an eligibility result is not a receipt. A prerequisite grants no operating or
action authority. Ordering/grouping remain separate unless a supported consuming
policy expressly declares a fact's semantic role.

Each local/parent decision closes one finite authoritative boundary with supported
relations, direction and reach, resolver/snapshot identity, coherent semantic-time
cut, inclusion/exclusion and cycle treatment. Account exactly once for each
matching relation and required role, including missing, duplicate, conflicting,
stale, superseded and unresolved facts. Mixed cuts and incomplete/unbounded
traversal refuse. Applicability's descendant cut is distinct from a parent's
closure-input cut; no universal transitive assessment rollup follows.

Structural containment cycles and closure-dependency cycles are distinct. Initial
support rejects both. Closure dependencies include parent-close requirements on
children and close-guarding prerequisites: a tree can still deadlock when a child
requires its own parent closed. Informational navigation cycles have no closure
effect, but cannot bypass same-occurrence evaluation causal rules. Operation timing
does not select a universal temporal planner; unsupported timing/cycle semantics
refuse rather than imply a scheduler.

Parent closure follows the policy's declared actual child-state/receipt roles and
the parent's own requirements. Where actual child closure is required, consume
authoritative current closed-state and/or the exact receipt linkage specified by
that role. A policy consuming child eligibility establishes only that proposition.
Neither children eligible nor parent eligible means parent closed. A state change
requires the parent's own congruent action authorization, admitted attempt,
through-commit enforcement and durable receipt. Child authority and assessments
cannot replace the parent's separate required authority or assessment.

## Selected revision, currentness, and history rules

Keep exact item, kind/composition, contract, policy, relation, subject, result,
authorization and receipt identities and revisions. Definition/default changes
do not silently rebind active or closed items. Where supported, explicit authorized
rebinding uses prior change terms and expected predecessor, preserves earlier
bindings, and forms new affected snapshots/closures. Unsupported rebinding refuses.
It never retargets an immutable old evaluation or reliance occurrence.

Pinned kind/contract versions do not freeze independently current governance.
Resolve affected assignment, membership, coverage and reliance at declared
checkpoints and preserve mandatory governing conditions and additional declared
dependencies through commit. Adding a required child/prerequisite after eligibility
requires new affected basis/result accounting; stale permission cannot authorize
the changed target. No silent migration or historical rewriting is allowed.

G5 retains both prospectively governed assessment and expressly adopted supported
retrospective qualification as protocol choices. The checkout study's prospective
production example was not selected as a universal rule. Retrospective use requires
a new current owner determination, independently justified requirements/protocol,
exact supported coverage, original provenance/authority, currentness and complete
populations including relevant failures. It cannot manufacture collection-time
review or import old evidence into a new occurrence.

New work begins at the earliest unsatisfied boundary. Changed requirements source,
intent or fixed evaluation inputs require a new Purpose/occurrence. Other changes
may require verification, qualification, assessment or observations as the exact
owner protocol specifies. Neither automatic whole-suite reruns nor automatic
old-result acceptance is selected. Historical results and receipts retain their
original meaning; current state and new uses require owner resolution, not replay
as present authority. Unrelated authored revisions do not invalidate everything.

## Discriminating scenarios

These paper scenarios illustrate selected distinctions. Names, exact policies,
states and operations are not executable definitions or additional selections.

1. **Plan, build, review, close.** Planning produces C@1 and actually closes under
   its own requirements. Endpoint candidate work A1 has its own bounded assessment
   and applicable security coverage before closure. UI work U1 may build after A1
   closes; its prerequisite on broader review S1 guards U1 close only. S1 inspects
   the exact endpoint and existing unfinished UI candidate, without requiring U1
   closed. After the required review and S1 closure, U1 can seek closure using its
   exact required roles, authorization and receipt path. Later candidate mutation
   requires affected coverage/currentness checks. Adding A1-close → S1-close while
   S1-close already requires A1 closed creates a rejected closure cycle. S1's review
   cannot replace the Feature's own integration assessment.
2. **Shared security work.** Two Features in one project require one security item
   Q through separate supported close prerequisites. Q retains one identity/state;
   grouping may show it in both views. Q's assessment covers only its declared
   subject and roles, so one Feature's integration requirements may still be
   unsatisfied. Claiming Q is contained by both Features is unsupported initially;
   duplicating Q or counting a progress percentage as eligibility is incorrect.
3. **Weaker composition or conflict.** A payment-scope item proposes a kind with
   API tests alone. Explicitly applicable security review remains required. Missing
   review causes formation refusal; a complete adverse review follows the named
   outcome policy. Conversely, retaining raw card numbers and prohibiting retention
   for the same use cannot be reconciled by a strictest/nearest-parent rule: refuse
   incompatible formation and seek an authorized supported correction/change.
4. **Changed basis and lossy adapter.** A parent eligible over two required children
   gains a third before commit. Preserve the earlier result; close the changed cut
   and stop the stale action. A future tracker showing only `done` may supply a
   disclosed view of its source Boolean. It cannot supply missing child closure,
   assessment coverage or native commit authority, even when an optional kind
   identifies the intended meaning.

## Adapter boundary and remaining choices

Future adapters must preserve meaning for each supported effect mapping, retain
exact identity/owner attribution, disclose lossy views and refuse unsupported
effects. Loss disclosure does not authorize a lossy mutation. Pack installation
grants no admission, activation, delegation or governing assignment under G6.
Mappings are selected individually; no universal adapter compatibility is promised.

Outside the subsequent [foundation selections](../../slices/work-item-state-foundation/README.md),
later bounded planning still selects physical applicability/action
owner placement and further work/definition realization; concrete states, operations,
reopen/cancel and removal consequences; exact policies, eligible populations,
compatibility/coverage mappings and assessment protocols; identity/resolver,
persistence, concurrency, recovery and retention mechanisms; schemas, serialization,
API/SDK composition, authoring/import and human views; consumer realization,
adapter families and slice/packet allocation. Subsequent bounded Engine/SDK
selections are owned by the [completed compositions/kinds authority](../../slices/work-compositions-and-kinds/README.md)
and [implemented baseline](../../implemented-baseline.md), rather than remaining
open for that delivered population. Examples settle none of the other choices.

Native owners and usable SDK capability remain first; new CLI, installed product
skills and adapters follow later. Existing roadmap order remains intact. The
[structured-data standard](../../structured-data-format-standard.md) still has
**PROPOSED general defaults**, with **Contracts JSON fixed** and existing
owner/versioned contracts controlling. This amendment selects no new format,
schema, layout, migration or implementation sequence. Subsequent planning and
execution require their own authorization. The delivered foundation and bounded
ordinary/planning-output/requirements-input composition/kind contracts are recorded
by those owning plans, not granted by this amendment.
