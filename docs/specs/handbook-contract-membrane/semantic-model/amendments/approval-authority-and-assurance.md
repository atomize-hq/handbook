## Independent dimensions

Approval is not one ladder of security levels. Resolve these dimensions separately
under the exact supported owner rules and prior approved terms:

| Dimension | Question and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Approval requirement | Does this action need a new decision, or do established rules and delegation already permit it? No new approval means existing authority permits the action; every operation still faces its owner checks. |
| Decision authority | May the implementation agent, a project-level agent, the human, or a designated role decide this exact choice? Role/title and additional context alone grant no permission. |
| Delivery channel | How is the decision carried: conversation, Substrate, or a future UI/messaging channel? Delivery does not establish decision authority or stronger identity assurance. |
| Identity assurance | What does the selected rule require and what is actually proved: local principal/delegation assertions, or a future supported credential method? A claim cannot satisfy a rule requiring independently verified facts. |
| Approval composition | Is one authorized approver sufficient, or does an expressly selected future rule require quorum or distinct roles? Composition is separate from transport and identity assurance; required review must actually occur. |

Initial project initiation, Charter/posture, governance or deliberate scoped
deferral, and operating terms still require human decisions. One coherent human
review may cover several separately attributable native owner outcomes. A proposal
cannot approve itself or authorize its own activation or wider delegation.

## Examples and future boundaries

- An initially delegated in-bounds payload correction may proceed without a new
  human decision, with new capture/occurrence and every required owner check.
- An implementation agent may submit an unresolved choice to a project-level
  agent with additional context and prior delegated authority. The native owner
  validates the exact decision. Reserved choices, unresolved material ambiguity
  or missing authority with no authorized resolution require human intervention.
- A wider-path proposal or a credential-required request with only local claims
  refuses. Conversation delivery, an agent label and a passing check supply no
  missing permission or assurance.

Prioritize verified reuse of Substrate delivery/context facilities while retaining
standalone Handbook and native validation. Host agent configurations are distinct
from Engine operating profiles/policies. General automatic routing and complete
Substrate adoption require their own causal justification and bounded plans.

A local PIN is an optional future direction. PIN design, MFA, device/provider
integration, messaging APIs, team quorum and a generalized approval framework
are not selected or prerequisites here. The
[slice](../../slices/independent-local-check/README.md) outlines the complete local
journey. Packets 1–3 retain delivered evidence; only
[Packet 4](../../slices/independent-local-check/packet-4.md) is now detailed for human
implementation-plan review. This cross-reference changes no approved owner rule.
