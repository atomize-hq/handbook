Missing retention cannot prove nonoccurrence. These matter to their specific claims, not universal permission to invoke Cargo.

6. **No demonstrated product operating envelope:** native recovery/packing correctness on earlier versions does not establish final online cleanup latency, accepted-tail boundedness under growth, authenticated fresh lookup bounds or full backup/restore at long history.
