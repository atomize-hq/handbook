## Findings

1. **[P1] Preserve the ownership-observed birth when acquiring a descriptor.** In suite_adapter.py:530-540, members() derives owned_pids from one process snapshot, then calls guard.process(pid) again and passes that fresh row to retain_birth. For a newly discovered descendant, there is no comparison between this row's start and snapshot[pid]['start']. If the original descendant exits, its parent reaps it, and the PID is reused between the snapshot and fresh read, the shared helper validates the replacement process's birth and fdinfo correctly, but it has validated the wrong owner. tracked/handles then retain that unrelated birth, and kill_owned() at 543-549 or the normal final cleanup can SIGKILL it. The new helper solves the race after its input row; it cannot recover the earlier ownership association that the caller discarded. The inner owned_snapshot() has the analogous numeric children-ID to fresh process-row handoff without checking that the observed new birth belongs to a verified owned parent. Bind acquisition to the row/birth that proved the ownership edge, or revalidate the current ownership edge and parent birth before committing the descriptor; do not replace an ownership-observed birth with an arbitrary fresh birth. This affects the outer operator used by finite8 and all other phases.

2. **[P2] Finish cleanup of retained processes when discovery becomes uncertain.** suite_adapter.py:644 and 652 call members() without catching its acquisition exception inside the phase finally block. retain_birth intentionally raises OwnedBirthUncertain on a disappearing process, failed pidfd_open or unverifiable fdinfo. A newly observed short-lived child can disappear between the process read and descriptor acquisition during failure cleanup. That exception then escapes finally before the existing descriptors are killed/reaped, raw readers and the phase observer are joined, and handles/memory streams are closed. The receipt correctly does not pass, but rejection alone does not finish ownership of other already-retained live children. Continue bounded termination/reaping/joining using the known handles even if a new discovery fails; retain the uncertainty and failed closure as evidence. Do not acquire an uncertain replacement or signal an unverified PID to compensate.

These are source-level reachable paths, not claimed runtime reproductions. The P1 was sent early to the parent while the full read continued. No candidate file was edited by this review.
