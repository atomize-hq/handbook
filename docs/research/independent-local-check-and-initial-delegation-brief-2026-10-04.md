## Snapshot, refusal and historical-result contracts

The selected question concerns **an exact captured payload and schema closure**. Bind those bytes/values to the declared subject and observation. Dock's baseline source is the exact request input (`request_ref#input/role`), whose revision is the complete inline input content fingerprint, not a Git/workspace revision. Separately, the source-currentness contract names that source identity/revision and observed current-head identity; its implementation must observe that authority, not echo the expected value. Conformance of a retained snapshot does not assert conformance of the latest workspace. If that stronger question is selected, capture and bind the actual later target through the supported route.
