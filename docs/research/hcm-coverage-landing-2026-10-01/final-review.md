table calls the time/currentness gap “Needs changing UTC-only assumptions.” C-15
correctly explains that the missing obligation is named owner/checkpoint and
admitted comparison closure, with clock technology deferred. An optional wording
change to “Needs changing owner-attributed time/comparison closure” would avoid
suggesting UTC itself is prohibited. The detailed claim and compact map already
preserve the correct boundary; no correctness or authority violation is present.
