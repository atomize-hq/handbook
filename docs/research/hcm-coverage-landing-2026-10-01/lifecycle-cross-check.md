# Cross-area lifecycle reconciliation

Baseline: `b0e73fc54f28d80c3f8f58d37b939db41bbbf0e4`, 2026-10-01.
This is the authorized cross-area research pass, not the controlling final review,
runtime certification, implementation authority, or a replacement specification.
Only this report was written. The [research brief](README.md) fixes scope and the
five area reports supply the detailed inventories; material conclusions below
also cite their original owning rules and inspected current paths.

**Conclusion:** the reports describe one compatible target lifecycle. Their narrow
implemented foundations do not establish the new closures merely because existing
records have fingerprints, currentness checks, or replay. No new unresolved semantic
decision or omitted owning subsection was identified in this pass. A retained
owning-summary wording discrepancy and one compact future-inventory summary need
the applicability qualification described in X-01 below. Concrete
allocation, policy contents, work model, physical owner placement and durability
mechanisms remain deferred implementation choices.

## Synthesis consequences and verification limits

Keep the five broad areas, with X-03's governing-assessment relationship shown as
an early dependency of governed preparation/commitment. Work/parent policy and
effect realization remain separately bounded future outcomes. Expose current
foundations and target gaps separately; avoid labeling a whole area covered or
discarding reusable primitives because their current public forms differ.
Assign historical resolution, public interfaces and refusal within every affected
increment rather than a final integration stage. The only report wording correction
requested here is F-11's X-01 qualification; retain X-01's approved-source wording
caveat visibly in planning input. R-02's earlier whole-capsule classification has
already been corrected. No new human semantic decision is identified by this pass.

Read all five area reports and future inventory, guide and register, owning heading/
scenario inventories and detailed lifecycle/current-use/pruning/history/action rules.
Followed current run preparation/readmission, finite Contracts/Engine exports and
policy registry, evidence append/snapshot/list/read, gate/local derivation and its
N/A/purity test, authority capsule publication fixture/read path and Charter
transaction guard/public receipt paths cited above. No runtime suite, producer,
external adopter, publication, host-enforcement or successful target-model consumer
check ran. Whole historical retention and live cross-owner availability are not
proved. GitNexus `query occurrence --repo handbook --limit 3` exited 0 with empty
degraded FTS results (missing Protocol/Category tables), foreign-worktree skip,
unsupported Node warning and 20-commit-stale index; no graph evidence was trusted or
reindex performed. No indexed symbol changed; root retains pre-commit detection.
