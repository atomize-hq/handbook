## Behavioral claims

**C-01 — Owner-issued occurrence versus content and delivery identity. Needs changing; owner lifecycle missing.** [Identity, 115–164](../../specs/handbook-contract-membrane/semantic-model/evidence-and-evaluation.md#evaluation-occurrence-and-purpose-identity) requires owner allocation within Purpose formation, a fingerprint excluding occurrence/record/delivery metadata, exact same-occurrence replay, changed-content refusal, and equal-content deliberate reruns with wholly separate downstream chains. E1/E8 instead expose caller nonce plus content-derived run reference that includes owner UTC/currentness; no retained formation request adjudicates conflicting occurrence reuse. Current exact-byte reconstruction is useful integrity checking, not this idempotency contract. Depends on complete requirements/subject/scope and admitted policy rules; **change needed** for F-13/F-14/F-16, concrete allocation/API **decision-dependent** F-17.

**C-02 — Stable selection and precommitted truth-affecting rules. Missing.** [Stable selection, 200–247](../../specs/handbook-contract-membrane/semantic-model/evidence-and-evaluation.md#stable-selection-and-checkpoint-current-facts) and Purpose 127–164 require exact role, producer/configuration, Dock/adapter, reach, capability, ceiling, prerequisite, retry/partial definitions, composition and Formation Policy/time/boundary rules before observations. E1 run fields, E6 protocol and E9 policies expose no complete Plan/Selection closure or distinct transient verification facts. Requested Resolution/manifest ceiling are narrower existing inputs. Depends on admission, requirements and definition owners; **fits** F-12/F-13, **change needed** F-14/F-16; concrete policies **decision-dependent** F-17.

**C-03 — Bounded pre-commit refresh and immutable one-basis commitment. Missing.** [Identity, 166–198](../../specs/handbook-contract-membrane/semantic-model/evidence-and-evaluation.md#evaluation-occurrence-and-purpose-identity) permits refreshed readiness/basis only with every fixed input unchanged and positively owner-verified; every prior basis remains immutable, unused and launch/commit-ineligible. First durable launch/refusal/enforcement/attempt/source/submission provenance commits one basis; replacement after commitment abandons/terminates without new truth and needs a new occurrence. E1 has read-only preparation; E3/E6 persist individual effects without an occurrence commitment register or accepted/unused basis states. Depends on C-01/C-02 and starting-evaluations readiness/Engine work; **change needed** F-13/F-14, persistence/concurrency choices **decision-dependent** F-17.

**C-04 — Later checks, bounded variation, recovery and refusal lineage. Verification retry is not execution retry. E5 current authority/source checks and E6 idempotent executor replay do not bind the governed selection/checkpoint/basis lineage or declare recovery/continuity rules. Depends on C-01–C-03 and preparation owner checks; **fits** F-13/F-15 integration boundaries, **change needed** F-14; policies **decision-dependent** F-17. HR-006 supplies settled meaning.
