This also resolves the old-report example. A new run establishing “report R contains a passing result” proves that bounded property of R. It does not establish “the actual change now satisfies its governing security requirements.” Independent governing selection and subject/coverage checks prevent that wrapper from becoming a completion substitute.

F. Proposed normative wording and targeted amendment map

The following wording assumes acceptance of the combined recommendations. It is not a statement that they are already normative.

Proposed owning clauses

Contract permitted use

Every admitted contract revision MUST have one exact governed permitted-use value: Diagnostic-only or Decision-seeking eligible. Diagnostic-only permits only diagnostic Evaluation Use Intent. Decision-seeking eligible permits diagnostic or decision_seeking, subject to all other formation, authorization, readiness, and use requirements.

Permitted use is semantic content owned by the Contracts contract-definition/admission responsibility. It is not a maturity score, admission status, readiness determination, governing assignment, evaluation outcome, or completion authority. No additional authored artifact family or independently mutable classification record is implied.

Qualification and change

Admission MUST establish the requested permitted use under the exact applicable project configuration and owner admission-rule basis, with complete candidate semantics and every required qualification input. A candidate, new name, prior pass, or requested run intent MUST NOT supply its own qualification authority. Missing or unverifiable qualification requirements refuse the affected admission.

Changing permitted use requires a new exact contract revision or successor and the applicable owner admission/lifecycle checks. Prior revisions, admission outcomes, evaluations, and diagnostic provenance remain unchanged. No diagnostic result automatically promotes a contract, and no mandatory diagnostic-to-decision progression is selected.

Formation and evaluation

Both requirement-selection sources MUST preserve the exact contract and its owner-established permitted use in the complete Evaluation Requirements closure. Evaluation Coordination MUST refuse incompatible requested use before forming a valid Evaluation Purpose for Engine authorization. Readiness cannot override this refusal, and the requested intent MUST NOT be silently changed.

Every later consuming closure MUST preserve the exact permitted-use and intent provenance. Class compatibility constrains valid formation and reliance; evaluators MUST NOT weaken claim, evidence, metric, or gate semantics according to class.

Posture-selected diagnostics and completion

An admitted Assurance Policy MAY resolve a diagnostic-only contract under its exact supported mapping. The resulting requirements remain diagnostic-only and cannot supply the mandatory assessment for positive governed completion. This permission introduces no implicit use-intent selector input or capability-driven fallback.

Positive governed completion MUST satisfy its mandatory assessment role through a decision-seeking evaluation of a decision-seeking-eligible contract selected through the applicable project posture and admitted assurance rules for the actual target/scope. Ordinary policy, exception, wrapper, child result, or display label MUST NOT waive that provenance requirement. Independent governing assignment, complete coverage, currentness, action authorization, and committed-effect checks remain necessary.

History and validation subjects

Exact earlier diagnostic results MAY inform authoring and historical rationale without becoming later-occurrence canonical evidence. Existing declared prior-fact roles and their reuse restrictions remain distinct from evidence-set import, which this decision does not add.

An admitted validation contract MAY assess an exact draft or provisional contract as data only through a declared compatible subject/input role. The validation contract’s permitted use governs that evaluation. The result establishes only its bounded validation proposition; admission, permitted-use qualification, and governing assignment of the inspected candidate require their separate owner decisions. No preview subsystem or universal recursive validation prerequisite is selected.
