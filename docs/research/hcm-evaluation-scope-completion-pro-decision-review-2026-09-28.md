### Recommendation: ADJUST—keep both evaluation paths, make governed completion an independently checked boundary

The two-path direction is sound. Direct evaluation should support incremental contract development without pretending to establish completion under the project’s posture. Posture-selected evaluation should remain the required assessment route for positive governed completion.

I would sign off on a narrow amendment that makes three points explicit:

Every evaluation has a complete, owner-closed requirements basis. Direct evaluation replaces posture selection with a different, explicitly owned source of requirements—not an optional requirements field.
The governing completion standard is resolved independently of the submitted development result. An admitted contract or completion policy is not automatically the one that governs the actual target.
The first-version diagnostic rule must be explicit. I recommend requiring both posture_selected provenance and decision_seeking intent in the mandatory completion-assessment role. That is an additional restriction beyond merely forbidding direct results, and it needs explicit acceptance.

The first two close the essential semantic gaps in the proposed direction. The third settles a genuine remaining choice in how that direction interacts with the existing diagnostic-policy permission.

This review is grounded in the audited Markdown snapshot. It does not establish executable behavior or inspect the locally updated bookkeeping. HR-001 remains closed.

### A. What is already established, and what is new?

The current contradiction remains exactly the one identified in P2-02: Evaluation Purpose makes Resolved Assurance Requirements conditional, while Readiness Basis, Prospective Evaluation Basis, occurrence formation, and final context require them unconditionally. Their definition supplies only a posture-selection source. Making that field optional would leave the direct path without an owner-established proof plan.




| Topic | Status and consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Complete accounting, exact admitted definitions, immutable occurrence and use intent, separate Engine authorization | Preserve existing requirements. Neither route weakens them. |
| A pass answers only its exact gate’s bounded question | Preserve. Complete claim accounting does not imply every claim passed, and a gate is not a completion decision. |
| Two requirement-selection sources with a complete direct source | New P2-02 decision. Requires an explicit semantic owner and common requirements contract. |
| Mandatory posture-selected assessment for governed completion | New, stronger downstream invariant. Existing action-specific policy permission alone does not establish it. |
| Independent applicability and selection of the project’s completion standard | Required closing refinement. Exact policy identity is insufficient when the caller can choose the wrong exact policy. |
| decision_seeking required for the mandatory completion assessment | My recommended additional first-version choice. Existing text permits policy-specific positive reliance on diagnostics. |
| Draft previews are not evaluations under an admitted draft contract; cross-occurrence evidence import remains unselected | Boundary clarification, not new preview or reuse functionality. |


The architecture’s main tradeoff is worthwhile: it permits small, meaningful development checks while preserving a separate project-completion standard. The cost is that a correct direct result cannot later be relabeled as the required project-selected assessment. Under the present evidence model, that can require new collection. Requiring decision_seeking for completion would impose the same deliberate restriction on a previously completed diagnostic assessment.

Change first: define the common requirements role and the independent completion-governance relationship. Only then normalize conditional wording and examples. Adding a source label without those responsibilities would document the bypass rather than close it.

### B. A complete direct-evaluation requirements basis
#### Keep Resolved Assurance Requirements posture-specific

I recommend introducing Evaluation Requirements as a common semantic role, fulfilled by either:

Posture-selected requirements: the existing exact Resolved Assurance Requirements produced by Contracts-domain Assurance Selection.
Direct-contract requirements: an exact requirements closure produced by a Contracts-domain direct requirement-resolution responsibility, consuming an explicitly nominated admitted contract and compatible admitted evaluation definitions.

The proposed direct responsibility belongs with Contracts’ definition-resolution semantics and remains distinct from policy admission, readiness comparison, and evidence evaluation. Evaluation Coordination consumes its output; it does not invent requirements while deciding what the available producers can attempt.

This assigns the missing responsibility without requiring another service, crate, repository, or standalone runtime record. The direct closure may be embedded in an existing owner-produced object, provided its exact content and identity are closed before consumption. The posture branch should reference its existing Resolved Assurance Requirements, not manufacture a second competing source.

The existing separation between Contracts-owned proof requirements and Evaluation Coordination’s later comparison against Engine authorization provides the appropriate boundary to extend.

#### Required content
| Element | Minimum semantic requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Subject and scope | Exact project, evaluation subject/snapshot, scope, and the owner-established bindings needed to interpret the contract against that subject. |
| Contract and complete inventory | One exact admitted, usable contract revision and semantic-content identity; its complete expected-claim inventory; contract-rooted claim-set references; and all required case, observation-role, and composition obligations. A claim-set reference cannot silently remove expected claims. |
| Evaluation meaning | Exact claim/applicability/evaluator definitions; every required compatible Evidence Evaluation Policy binding; the complete selected metric inventory and configurations; one exact Gate Policy; and all dependencies needed to interpret them. An explicitly empty metric inventory is different from unresolved metric selection. |
| Selection provenance | The selection source, exact source owner and closure, nomination or resolution basis, all relevant admitted definitions and currentness observations, and complete selected/excluded/missing/conflicting accounting. |
| Closure rules | Exact formation and truth-affecting boundary/time rules must be precommitted at their existing required stages. They cannot be filled in after observations reveal which alternative is favorable. |
| Explanation | Why these exact requirements were selected, what they cover, and what they do not establish. Posture-selected provenance retains the entire existing snapshot/selector/policy/exception chain. |


For direct evaluation, the caller may nominate the contract and permitted evaluation bindings. The owner verifies completeness and compatibility; the caller’s submission is not the closure. Missing required bindings refuse formation rather than becoming defaults, unknown readiness, or a reduced claim inventory.

A smaller contract is acceptable as a separately admitted definition. It is complete relative to that contract, not automatically complete relative to the project’s actual delivery obligation. A later revision or a different configured evaluation rule changes the evaluation’s meaning and identity; it does not rewrite the earlier result.

The existing posture-selected output already fixes one contract, its complete inventory, compatible evidence policies, configured metrics, and a gate. The common role should preserve that level of completeness in both branches.

#### How every stage consumes it

The common sequence becomes:

Evaluation Requirements → prospective applicability, when used → Observation Plan → Evaluation Purpose and occurrence → Engine authorization → readiness → Prospective Evaluation Basis → collection/admission/frontier/evidence closure → final context → claim/metric/gate truth.

Universal references that mean “the proof obligations for this evaluation” should consume Evaluation Requirements. References specifically describing posture resolution or Assurance Selection should continue to require Resolved Assurance Requirements.

The selection source—proposed names posture_selected and direct_contract—and its exact provenance participate in the Purpose’s semantic content and fingerprint. They remain transitively bound through authorization, collection, final context, results, and explanation. Changing the source creates a new occurrence even when the contract and evaluation rules are identical.

Use intent remains the separate decision_seeking/diagnostic distinction. The existing readiness table continues to apply unchanged to both sources. Neither source supplies Engine permission, producer capability, or evidence.

A failed or held project-selected attempt must not automatically fall back to a direct attempt under the same Purpose. A deliberately separate development evaluation may proceed when otherwise admissible; it does not clear the original governed obligation.

### C. Closing the completion bypass mechanically
#### Establish the governing relationship—not merely an admitted policy

The critical new requirement is an owner-established governing-completion binding. This is a semantic relationship, not a mandated new record type.

For the actual project, action, target, and scope, it must establish:

whether the requested effect discharges a project-governed completion, promotion, or release obligation;
which exact completion/reliance policy and policy-selection basis govern;
which Assurance Policy Selector, selection basis, and Posture Scope establish the required assessment;
the exact evaluation-subject-to-action-target relationship;
the relevant work/parent/dependency boundary and currentness requirements.

Work Progression’s policy/lifecycle responsibility owns this relationship for local and parent completion. The corresponding action domain owns it for release or another separately governed effect. The authoritative work-state/structure owner supplies actual target and relationship facts. Engine supplies posture; Contracts Assurance Selection supplies the resolved requirements. No one reconstructs another owner’s proposition.

This extends existing ownership: Work Progression already owns action-specific policy selection and target closure, while consuming rather than inventing authoritative work-state and relationship facts.

The governing relationship must be established through the relevant owner’s authorized definition/lifecycle boundary, independently of the development result. Admission into an available policy population and assignment as the governing policy are different propositions, even when one owner operation establishes both.

HR-001 does not automatically supply this relationship. It establishes governance over policy admission. It does not declare every admitted policy applicable to every completion request. Its accepted distinction between admission history and present usability remains intact.

Concrete project mappings can remain unselected in the package, but the consequence cannot: without an exact, applicable governing relationship, the affected governed-completion operation refuses. Absence must not mean “development mode,” “ungoverned,” or “choose any admitted policy.”

#### Enforce at the relevant owner operations
| Boundary | Required check |
| --- | --- |
| Completion-policy/relationship admission and lifecycle | The policy’s applicability, effect, scope, and allowed result roles preserve the mandatory completion boundary. A definition cannot waive it by naming the effect “development completion.” |
| Project-selected evaluation formation | Resolve the actual governing target/scope and designated assurance-selection basis before collection. Form the assessment from that exact selection, not from a previously favorable direct result. This requires no future completion Determination or Action Intent. |
| Operational Reliance formation/evaluation | Independently resolve current governing applicability; verify the submitted assessment’s source, use intent, exact requirements, historical validity, and target coverage; account for every required result and dependency. |
| Action authorization and attempt admission | Require the exact eligible Determination and congruent target/effect. Broader advisory or verified authority cannot repair a missing qualifying assessment. |
| Committed completion state | The authoritative state owner verifies the exact authorized effect, expected target, and governing conditions that must remain applicable through commit. A generic status-setting route cannot create the same governed-completion proposition without these checks. |
| Projection | Preserve the bounded evaluation result and distinguish it from completion eligibility and committed completion. Prose or a displayed pass cannot substitute for owner results. |


The existing action model already fixes exact action questions, prohibits ambient convenient-policy selection, and separates reliance, authorization, and committed effect. The amendment adds mandatory completion-specific inputs and predicates to those boundaries; it does not move them into the gate or CLI.


#### Prevent policy and scope shopping

A caller may request a different bounded development check. That does not let the caller determine what counts as completion of the original work.

A legitimately narrower child task may have its own allowed developmental state or bounded closeout. Its result must remain a proposition about that child. It cannot discharge a parent’s required assessment, omit a required relationship, or convert “child eligible” into “child actually closed.” Parent completion independently applies its governing requirements over the authoritative parent boundary. The current parent rules already require exact relationship accounting and distinguish eligibility from actual closure.

Likewise, an admitted higher-order policy-selection rule cannot evade the new floor by selecting a convenient weaker completion policy. Its selected policy must still be the one authorized and applicable to the actual governed action.

One limitation must remain explicit: the model cannot infer requirements that project governance never encoded. If the genuinely governing, authorized selection incorrectly chooses an inadequate contract, exact provenance alone does not prove that contract is a good engineering standard. This decision prevents substitution and undeclared weakening; it does not replace the substantive task of defining adequate project requirements. Nor can it prohibit a genuinely authorized governance change merely because that change reduces a configurable requirement.

#### Currentness at completion

For governed completion, I recommend making the governing applicability, target coverage, posture-selection basis, and required assessment relationship mandatory present-use and through-commit dependencies, not optional policy inputs.

An existing posture-selected assessment can be used while that exact basis remains applicable. “Fresh project-selected evaluation” does not mean rerun for every completion request; it means the earlier direct occurrence cannot become that assessment.

A change to the applicable posture snapshot, selector, selected contract/rules, or exception basis requires a fresh selected evaluation when it changes the required evaluation closure. A change only to the completion policy requires a new reliance decision and a fresh evaluation only when the new requirements or acceptance relation demand one. Merely publishing an unused contract revision does not invalidate everything.

Fresh verification of the same exact applicable basis is not substitution of a new basis. This is a downstream verification distinction, not a resolution of P2-04’s collection-lifecycle question.

These completion-governance dependencies also do not make the historical Engine Evaluation Authorization Binding universally continuing-current. Its two existing reliance modes remain separate.

### D. Diagnostic results and positive completion

The current preparation text permits positive reliance on diagnostics when an exact policy explicitly allows it. The action chapter makes that permission specific to the result role, direction, and action question. Neither presently creates the proposed mandatory project-completion restriction.


My first-version recommendation is this matrix:

| Requirement-selection source | Use intent | May fill the mandatory positive governed-completion assessment role? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct contract | decision_seeking | No. May support bounded development or other expressly permitted, non-substituting reliance. |
| Direct contract | diagnostic | No. Diagnostic provenance and action-specific restrictions remain. |
| Posture-selected | diagnostic | No, under my recommended additional restriction. Valid truth and permitted remediation uses remain available. |
| Posture-selected | decision_seeking | Potentially. Exact governing applicability, requirements, target coverage, outcome, and all other reliance/action checks still apply. |


Requiring decision_seeking here is stricter than prohibiting diagnostic relabeling. A fully valid posture-selected diagnostic pass would still not fill this role. That cost should be accepted deliberately.

The restriction belongs to the mandatory completion role, not to every positive operational decision. A diagnostic failure may legitimately produce eligible / allow for a remediation action. That positive eligibility means “consider this remediation,” not “the original work is complete.” The existing diagnostic-remediation scenario remains valid.

Ordinary policy and typed exceptions may not waive this source/use floor. Properly authorized exceptions may still affect the selected requirements within their existing permitted scope; they cannot turn a direct result into posture-selected provenance or a diagnostic occurrence into decision-seeking provenance.

Missing or incompatible required assessment provenance should produce Reliance Refusal, not a fabricated completion allow, and not a rewritten gate. A valid qualifying assessment with an unfavorable semantic outcome is handled by the exact completion policy’s existing non-positive decision rules. Supplemental development results may be consumed in permitted roles, but cannot substitute for—or be wrapped into a substitute for—the mandatory assessment.

### E. What can be promised about evidence reuse?

The current model does not provide a general direct-to-project-selected evidence-reuse route.

It requires new occurrence-bound collection for new evaluation truth, rejects evidence belonging to another occurrence or prospective basis, and expressly defers cross-occurrence evidence import or reuse. A claim-level Evidence Evaluation Policy may select only within the already closed evidence set; it cannot create an import route.


The safe commitments are:

Existing exact definitions may be resolved again. Reusing an admitted contract, evaluator, metric definition, or policy as an input is not transferring an earlier execution’s evidence.

Historical results remain readable and replayable. They may support explicitly permitted action-specific reliance, subject to the new completion restriction. Historical replay does not become a new assessment.

A project-selected occurrence must obtain its own valid authorization, prospective basis, occurrence-bound collection, evidence closure, and result. Equal contract content, equal subjects, fresh-looking timestamps, or new wrapper identifiers do not transfer the old evidence.

This does not imply that every external test ever run during development must be repeated. The selected proof plan determines the required observations. Tests outside it are not automatically required; independently established pre-existing facts remain usable only in the roles the existing model actually permits. But when a required observation establishes execution under the new occurrence and no supported reuse semantics exists, that execution must occur again.

An old report can be the subject of a new admitted validation—for example, checking its structure. That proves the newly observed property of the report, not automatically that the current implementation passed the original tests. Creating a new observation saying “this report says pass” is not a legitimate way to transfer the old execution’s evidentiary authority.

A future reusable-test-evidence mechanism would need a separate proposal addressing original and new occurrence lineage, authorization, subject correspondence, permitted semantic roles, freshness, complete accounting, and non-duplication. This amendment should neither add that mechanism nor promise its eventual availability.

### F. Proposed wording, impact map, and discriminating examples

The following text expresses the recommended resolution, including the explicitly proposed decision_seeking completion restriction.

#### Proposed normative text

Common requirements and source ownership

Every canonical evaluation MUST consume one complete, exactly identified Evaluation Requirements closure. Its requirement-selection source MUST be either posture-selected or direct-contract. Posture-selected requirements are the exact Resolved Assurance Requirements produced by Assurance Selection. Direct-contract requirements are closed by the Contracts-domain direct requirement-resolution responsibility from an exact admitted contract and a complete set of compatible admitted evaluation definitions.

The closure MUST account for the exact subject/snapshot and scope, contract revision, complete expected-claim and required observation inventory, applicability and evaluator definitions, required Evidence Evaluation Policy bindings, configured metrics, Gate Policy, and their dependencies. It MUST preserve exact source-owner, nomination/resolution, admission/currentness, and selection-accounting provenance. Required formation and truth-affecting boundary/time rules remain precommitted at their declared stages. Missing, incompatible, ambiguous, or substituted requirements refuse formation; they do not select a smaller plan.

Identity and consumption

Evaluation Coordination and all later consumers MUST preserve the same exact requirements and requirement-selection provenance. Posture-specific consumers additionally retain the complete Resolved Posture Snapshot, selector, policy-resolution, selected-policy, exception, and Resolved Assurance Requirements lineage. Requirement-selection source and Evaluation Use Intent are distinct, immutable semantic inputs. Changing either or changing the bound requirements requires a new Purpose and occurrence; equal contract references or outcomes do not permit relabeling.

Direct evaluation MAY assess a smaller separately admitted contract. It MUST NOT omit claims from that contract, weaken an already selected proof plan, or be substituted for a held or refused project-selected assessment. These distinctions do not require an additional standalone runtime record or repository.

Governing completion applicability

Before evaluating positive governed-completion eligibility, Work Progression or the responsible action domain MUST independently resolve the owner-established relationship among the exact project, action effect, actual target/scope, applicable completion policy and selection basis, required Assurance Policy Selector/Posture Scope, and authoritative work/dependency boundary. Admission of a policy into a candidate population does not by itself establish that governing relationship.

Applicability and governing-policy selection MUST NOT derive from the submitted result’s direction, development label, caller-preferred contract, or an unverified narrowing of the work. Missing, conflicting, or unverifiable governing inputs refuse the affected completion operation; they do not establish exemption.

Mandatory completion assessment

A positive determination that discharges a project-governed completion, promotion, or release obligation MUST satisfy its required assessment role using posture-selected, decision-seeking evaluation truth formed under the applicable governing requirements for the actual target/scope. Direct-contract or diagnostic results MUST NOT satisfy that role directly or through relabeling, wrapping, child-result substitution, or policy-selected waiver. Ordinary policies and exceptions cannot disable this provenance floor.

A qualifying assessment is necessary, not sufficient: the exact completion policy, complete dependency accounting, current target relationship, separate action authorization, and committed-effect rules still apply. Other permitted development, diagnostic, remediation, and non-completion uses retain their action-specific semantics.

Change and effect boundaries

Governing completion applicability and required assessment coverage MUST be verified at reliance and preserved through the completion commit boundary. A change that invalidates that relationship stops the affected reliance or action; affected owner results are formed anew without rewriting historical truth. A narrower child result cannot discharge a parent obligation absent that parent’s complete governing determination. Every supported operation establishing the same governed-completion proposition is subject to this boundary regardless of its name or interface.

Changes to posture, policy applicability, contracts, completion rules, and typed exceptions follow their separately authorized owner lifecycles. Development intent supplies no additional change authority. Historical Engine authorization verification and its declared continuing-status mode remain distinct from current completion-governance checks.

Authoring, reuse, and presentation

Exploratory execution or preview of an unadmitted draft is not canonical evaluation under that draft contract and does not admit it. This distinction selects no preview subsystem.

A new project-selected occurrence does not retroactively adopt a direct occurrence’s authorization, observations, evidence, or result. Existing occurrence, evidence-closure, and reuse restrictions remain unchanged.

Owner results and projections MUST preserve requirement-selection source, use intent, and the bounded meaning of a pass. A prose completion claim is not an Operational Reliance Determination or a committed completion result. Advisory authority remains permitted where the governing rule allows it, without fabricated identity verification or host enforcement.
